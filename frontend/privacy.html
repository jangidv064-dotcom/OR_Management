Privacy Policy

Open Rails Act Manager

This policy explains how your information is handled when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you register and use Open Rails Act Manager, we may collect the following information:

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Date of birth.
  • Encrypted password hash.
  • Activities and information saved in your account.
  • Technical information necessary to operate and secure the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage user accounts.
  • To verify age eligibility.
  • To process account approval requests.
  • To provide access to saved Open Rails activities.
  • To maintain platform security.
  • To send important account-related notifications.
  • To troubleshoot technical issues.

3. Password and Account Security

Passwords are stored as password hashes rather than plain-text passwords.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect account information. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

4. Storage of User Activities

Activities and related information that you save are associated with your account.

Your saved activities are intended to remain accessible to you when you log in using your account.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with service providers necessary to operate the platform, such as database hosting and email delivery providers.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or valid legal requests.

6. Cookies and Sessions

The platform may use session cookies to maintain your login status and protect authenticated access.

These cookies help the platform function properly.

7. Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain account records, meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.

Account deletion and data deletion requests will be handled subject to applicable legal requirements.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request:

  • Access to your personal information.
  • Correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion of personal information where applicable.
  • Information about how your data is used.
  • Withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent.

9. Third-Party Services

The platform may rely on third-party services for hosting, database management and email delivery. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the platform's functionality, data practices or applicable legal requirements change.

Important changes will be communicated where required by applicable law.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, access requests or data deletion requests, contact the platform administrator through the available support channel.
